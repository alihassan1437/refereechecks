Radical labelling

Every reference states exactly what it is

This is the heart of RefereeChecks. An employer can always reconstruct what the referee did — and didn’t do — and when.

Updated for this role

The referee edited or added answers inside this request’s window, for this specific role and employer.

“Updated {date} for {Role} at {Employer} · Standing reference originally collected {date}.”

Confirmed for this role

The referee reviewed the standing reference and actively released it unchanged for this application. An active confirmation is a stronger signal than silence — so it gets its own label.

“Referee reviewed and confirmed without changes on {date} for this application.”

Standing reference

The referee didn’t act inside the window, so the reference they gave earlier was released under their standing authorisation — and it says so, in those words.

“Referee notified of this application on {date} and did not update or withdraw it · Released under referee’s standing authorisation.”

Please note: a RefereeChecks reference is not a worker screening check, police check, or working-with-children check, and does not satisfy any obligation under the Aged Care Act 2024 (Cth) or the NDIS worker screening rules. It speeds up the reference step that sits alongside those mandated checks — it never replaces them.

Trust & safety

A reference is only worth something if it can be trusted

RefereeChecks is designed so a hiring manager never has to wonder what they’re looking at — and so workers and referees stay in control of their own information.

Referees are ID-checked

Every referee passes a document identity check with liveness before any reference can be delivered — and we label exactly how each referee was verified, never more than the evidence supports.

Employers are ABN-verified

Only verified businesses can request references, checked against the Australian Business Register and — for care providers — the sector registers.

The candidate authorises every release

References are disclosed only against a specific application the candidate has approved. No browsing, no talent-pool searching, no speculative pulls — ever.

Referees are notified on every use

Every request and every delivery is logged and visible. Referees can revoke a reference at any time, and candidates can see exactly who has viewed their passport.

No cherry-picking

References for an employment travel together — all of them or none. A passport can’t hide an awkward reference, which is precisely why employers can rely on it.

We never sell data

A published commitment: no sale, rent or exchange of reference, referee or candidate data — to anyone, for any price, in any form. No advertising, no profiling.

FAQ

Straight answers

Does RefereeChecks replace a police check or NDIS Worker Screening?

No — never. A RefereeChecks reference is not a worker screening check, police check, or working-with-children check, and does not satisfy any obligation under the Aged Care Act 2024 (Cth) or the NDIS worker screening rules. Those mandated checks remain the provider’s responsibility. RefereeChecks speeds up the reference step that sits alongside them.

What happens if my referee doesn’t respond?

Your application isn’t held hostage. The employer already has your verified facts instantly. When the 3-business-day window closes, your standing reference is delivered automatically and labelled plainly: the referee was notified and did not update or withdraw it. If the referee couldn’t be contacted at all, that’s labelled too — and if no notification reached them, only the factual layer is released.

Can I see what my referee wrote about me?

Yes. You can read everything stored in your passport, see every request and every delivery, add a response statement that travels with the reference, and ask for provably wrong facts to be corrected. We’re upfront about the trade: referees write knowing you can read it, which trades a little candour for a lot of speed and verifiability.

Can I hide a reference I don’t like?

No. References for an employment travel together — all of them or none — and a completeness indicator shows employers the full record. That’s deliberate: it’s what makes a passport more trustworthy than a testimonial you picked yourself. You can add referees (up to three per employment) and you can respond to anything in writing.

I’m a referee. Why would I bother — and does it cost me anything?

It costs you nothing, ever. In return you write each reference once instead of filling in a fresh 10–20 minute form for every employer who asks. Later requests arrive pre-filled: one tap to confirm, a few minutes to edit. You’re notified every time your reference is used, you can revoke it at any time, and you’ll hear when the person you backed gets the job.

How do you know a referee is real?

Two separate checks. Identity: the referee passes a document identity check with liveness before anything they write can be delivered. Affiliation: we establish they actually held the role at that employer — through their work email at the employer’s domain, employer or HR confirmation, or a reviewed professional-profile corroboration. Every reference shows its verification level and how it was established. Someone pretending to be your supervisor with a free email account can’t get through.
